Week 7: Text Indexing

How can we search a collection without reading every text again?

Week Content

We build a small text-search system, from an inverted index to Boolean queries and two-word phrase search.

  • Inverted indexes, vocabulary and postings
  • Preparing text and building an index in Python
  • Single-term and Boolean queries: AND, OR and AND NOT
  • Positional indexes and two-word phrase search

Materials

Lecture slides - PDF Lab notebook - IPYNB

Lab

Part A - Guided: repeat the classroom code on the same eight documents, D1-D8, and compare the outputs with the lecture examples.

Part B - Independent: apply the same functions to the 5,000 texts included in the notebook. Choose queries, compare the results, read matching texts and try a phrase search.

Part C: answer three short questions about your results. One or two sentences per answer are enough. No separate data file is needed.

Lab Checkpoint
