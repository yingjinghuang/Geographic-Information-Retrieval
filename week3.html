Week 3: Geoparsing II

How well does a geoparser find place names?

Week Content

We compare predicted place names with annotated examples, then look at individual successes and errors.

  • Ground truth, true positives, false positives and false negatives
  • Precision, recall and F1 score
  • Inspecting individual predictions and missed place names
  • The effects of context, language and model size
