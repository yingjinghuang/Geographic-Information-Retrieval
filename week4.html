Week 4: Geocoding I

Which location does a place name refer to?

Week Content

After recognising a place name, we need to connect it to a location. Geocoding turns a name into a geographic position.

  • Toponym resolution and gazetteer lookup
  • Geocoding with GeoPy and web services
  • Place-name ambiguity and contextual clues
  • Evaluating results with mean and median error distance
