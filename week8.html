Week 8: Spatial Indexing

How can spatial queries avoid checking every point?

Week Content

We start from a geographic query and build the spatial-search workflow step by step, from exact distance checks to a reusable R-tree spatial index.

  • Separating a query into text and spatial conditions
  • Direct distance search and exact within-radius filtering
  • Bounding-box candidate filtering
  • R-tree hierarchy, bounding boxes and STR grouping
  • GeoPandas .sindex and reusing one index across repeated queries

Materials

Lecture slides - PDF Lab notebook - IPYNB Final project and proposal details

Lab

Part A - Guided: repeat the six-document castles near Vienna example from the lecture. You will apply the text condition, direct distance search, bounding-box filtering and a GeoPandas spatial index.

Part B - Independent: apply the same workflow to 80 supplied Vienna POIs for the query cafés near the query point. Start with a 500 m radius, then change it to 750 m while reusing the same spatial index. Each task points you back to the relevant Guided section for a hint.

Part C - Interpretation: answer three short questions using your own Part B outputs. Focus on the candidate count, exact spatial matches, final café matches, and what changes when the radius increases.

All data needed for the lab are included in the notebook. The optional visualization cells are for understanding only and are not required for submission.

Final Project Workshop

The second part of the class starts the final project. Teams develop an idea, exchange peer feedback and finish with a revised plan and clear next tasks.

  • Project directions: system design, a specific GIR technique, spatial analysis, or evaluation. These directions can overlap.
  • Proposal: one 1-2 page team proposal before your Week 9 class. The proposal is worth 15% of the course grade.
  • Proposal sections: Project Aim and Context; Data and Materials; Proposed Approach and Analysis; Expected Outcomes; Work Plan and Feasibility.
  • Final project: worth 30%. The team presents in Week 11, every member speaks, and the final materials include slides, code and data.
