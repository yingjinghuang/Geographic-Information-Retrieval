Week 2: Geoparsing I

How can we recognise place names in text?

Week Content

Geoparsing identifies references to places in text. This week introduces the task and the approaches used to find those references.

  • Place names and named entity recognition (NER)
  • Tokenization, part-of-speech tagging and gazetteer lookup
  • Rule-based and machine-learning approaches
  • Using spaCy to inspect tokens and named entities
