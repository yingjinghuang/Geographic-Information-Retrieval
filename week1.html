Week 1: Introduction

What makes search geographic?

Week Content

This week introduces the course and the basic ideas behind Information Retrieval (IR) and Geographic Information Retrieval (GIR).

  • Documents, queries, information needs, relevance, and ranking
  • When geographic meaning changes a retrieval task
  • The GIR pipeline: geoparsing → geocoding → text/spatial indexing → ranking
  • Course Jupyter workflow and the first retrieval notebook
